# @brilliantwithashley

**Platforms:** Instagram (@brilliantwithashley)
**Niche:** College study-tips / studytok content — a direct competitor in the user's educational-content niche
**Why the user saves them:** Named explicitly as "one in my niche and a popular competitor"; liked that she has "multiple things in front of her original video" — small illustrative images stamped over her continuous talking-head take.

## Signature vibe
Rapid-fire numbered listicle delivered in one continuous, uncut talking-head take. A persistent pinned title stays on screen for the whole video, with terse outlined captions and a small (~40% width) illustrative photo swapping in over her chest for each numbered point. All "editing" lives in the overlay layer — the cheapest possible production model, and the fastest pacing (~1.5s/beat) of the creators studied so far.
