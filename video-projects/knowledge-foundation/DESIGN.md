# DESIGN — Knowledge Is The Foundation

## Style Prompt
Fresh, futuristic, "Google-like" motion graphics: a deep midnight canvas lit by crisp glowing primaries.
Clean geometric type, rounded shapes, liquid-glass UI cards, playful die-cut stickers that pop with
bounce. Every scene has drifting light, a faint grid and grain. Three worlds: the field, the office,
the operating room. They come together on one foundation: knowledge.

## Colors (each one has a meaning)
| Role | Hex | Meaning |
|------|-----|---------|
| Canvas | `#0a0e1a` | Midnight stage (never pure black) |
| Ink | `#f5f8ff` | Headlines (chrome-white gradient + halo) |
| Blue | `#4285f4` | Knowledge / the thread through every scene |
| Green | `#34a853` | The field / training / growth |
| Yellow | `#fbbc05` | Business / career / ambition |
| Red | `#ea4335` | Surgery / life / stakes |

The four primaries show up together only on the final foundation, where every world meets.

## Typography
- Display: **Outfit** 800, tight tracking, ALL CAPS headlines
- HUD / labels: **JetBrains Mono** 500–700

## Motion
- Words slam in from scale 1.8 + blur. Stickers pop with `back.out(2.4)` and a wiggle.
- Transitions: ball-into-camera flash → vertical blur whip → surgical-light overexposure.
- Final 4.5s holds on the foundation and the voiceover.

## What NOT to Do
- No flat white text and no hard cuts
- No logos or wordmarks of real companies, only the palette mood
- No more than these 4 accent colors
- No static frames: something always drifts
